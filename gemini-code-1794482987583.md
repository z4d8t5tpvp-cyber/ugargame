## 3. MATHEMATICAL & LEGAL ENGINE (RUSSIAN MARKET REALISM 2026–2027)

Implement these exact formulas in `MarketAndComplianceEngine.ts`:

### 3.1. Marat KMI's True Net Profit Formula (Unit Economics)[span_20](start_span)[span_20](end_span)
For any trade cycle with input capital $V_{RUB}$, buy price $P_{buy}$, and sell price $P_{sell}$:
1. $Q_{USDT} = \frac{V_{RUB} - F_{SBP\_in}}{P_{buy}} \cdot (1 - f_{maker1})$
2. $Q_{after\_gas} = \max(0, Q_{USDT} - C_{gas\_USDT})$ (where $C_{gas} = 0$ for intra-exchange, $0.15\text{ USDT}$ for `TON`, $1.50\text{ USDT}$ for `TRC-20`).
3. $R_{gross\_RUB} = Q_{after\_gas} \cdot (1 - f_{maker2}) \cdot P_{sell} - F_{SBP\_out}$
4. $\text{PnL}_{before\_tax} = R_{gross\_RUB} - V_{RUB}$
5. $\text{TaxReserve} = \max(0, \text{PnL}_{before\_tax} \cdot \tau)$ (where $\tau = 0.13$ for `3-НДФЛ` with documented expense receipts, or $0.15$ for `ИП УСН Доходы минус расходы`)[span_21](start_span)[span_21](end_span).
6. $\text{NetProfit}_{RUB} = \text{PnL}_{before\_tax} - \text{TaxReserve}$[span_22](start_span)[span_22](end_span).

### 3.2. Bank Anti-Fraud Scoring (`115-ФЗ` & `161-ФЗ`)
Each bank account tracks `riskScore115` ($0..100$):
- Increases when: money leaves within $<20$ minutes of arriving (`Instant Transit +6.5`), monthly turnover exceeds `600 000 ₽` without premium tier, `Retail Spend Ratio` (groceries/utilities) is $<10\%$, or trading at night (`+3.0`).
- Thresholds:
  - `riskScore115 >= 50`: 25% chance of **24-hour Cooling Hold (`Период охлаждения`)**.
  - `riskScore115 >= 72`: **Compliance Request (`Запрос документов по 115-ФЗ`)** — player has 5 days to attach saved PDF receipts from `Папка: Чеки и Ордера` and a tax statement from **Grisha the Accountant**[span_23](start_span)[span_23](end_span).
  - `riskScore115 >= 90`: **Full `115-ФЗ` Account Restriction (`Ограничение ДБО п. 11 ст. 7 115-ФЗ`)**.
  - Accepting a 3rd-party transfer without appeal triggers **`161-ФЗ` (`База ЦБ РФ`)**, freezing ALL bank apps until resolved via CBR appeal with Grisha the Accountant (takes 15 days and legal fees)[span_24](start_span)[span_24](end_span).
